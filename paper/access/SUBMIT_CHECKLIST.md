# IEEE Access Submission Checklist

## Before you start (one-time, ~15 min)
1. Create an ORCID at https://orcid.org (free). MANDATORY for you as
   corresponding author. Make the profile PUBLIC and fill in: name,
   The Harker School, and this paper's field. Note the ORCID iD.
2. Create an IEEE account (free) if you don't have one.
3. Optional but useful: decide on the author photo. The manuscript
   currently uses the no-photo bio block; if you want a photo, send me
   one (>= 100x120 px) and I'll switch the bio block.

## Files in this folder, ready to upload
- main_access.pdf ............ manuscript, official IEEE Access template
- main_access.tex + cls/bst/figs ... source files (portal wants source AND
  matching PDF; zip this whole folder minus this checklist if asked)
- graphical_abstract.jpg ..... 660x295, 27KB (spec-compliant; portal may
  ask for it now or at acceptance)
- cover_letter.md ............ paste into the cover-letter field

## Portal steps
1. Go to https://ieee.atyponrex.com/journal/ieee-access (linked from
   ieeeaccess.ieee.org/authors/submission-guidelines/)
2. Sign in, start new submission, article type: regular research article
3. Enter title/abstract/index terms (copy from main_access.pdf)
4. Author: you, sole author, The Harker School, mnn@yogins.com, ORCID
5. Upload main_access.pdf + source zip + graphical abstract
6. Cover letter: paste from cover_letter.md
7. Suggested/opposed reviewers: leave blank unless asked; if suggested
   reviewers are required, tell me and I'll compile a fair list from the
   cited literature
8. Attest originality / exclusive submission (true: nothing else is
   currently submitted)
9. Submit. All later correspondence arrives via the portal + email.

## Money and expectations (talk to your parents BEFORE acceptance)
- APC: $2,160 + tax, charged ONLY if accepted. No fee to submit or to be
  rejected. No clear hardship-waiver route for unaffiliated authors; you
  could email the editorial office to ask before or during review.
- First decision typically ~4-6 weeks. IEEE Access is binary
  accept/reject (no long revision cycles); technically-sound work is the
  bar. If rejected, IJHSR/JoCE remain open (one venue at a time).

## Fine print
- AI disclosure is in the Acknowledgment section naming the systems used,
  per IEEE policy. Do not remove it.
- The repo goes public when the paper is accepted (the manuscript
  promises code availability on publication).
- You are under 18: IEEE has no stated policy; the account is yours. If
  the portal asks for anything unexpected (consent, affiliation letters),
  stop and tell me.
